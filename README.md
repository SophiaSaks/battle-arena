# Welcome to our BattleArena game GitHub!

### *You have taken the right step into a magical fantasy world of battles amongst warriors and wizards! Fight battles against the computer in 1v1 battles and rise through the ranks of the gladiators. Will you come out victorious and be crowned the BattleArena Gladiator Champion? Download now, and begin your journey!*

## Background
This is a group project we did together for a school project in OOP in Java. 

## Setup
This is a console app, so all you need to do is:
* Clone git repo
* Start Server and Main
* It runs on port 5001

## Features
* Pick between 4 different characters
* Play a fair battle against computer (first turn is randomized)
* Watch battle history

What more do you want? Candy? **There will be no candy.**

## Characters:
You can choose between 4 characters:
* Knight: Defend with your shield! 120 HP
* Magician (not Mage) : Heal yourself! 150 HP
* Dragon: Breathe fire on your enemies! 80 HP
* Ranger: Dodge attacks! 100 HP
